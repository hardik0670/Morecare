The Morecare Ergo-Air Seat Cushion represents the peak of ergonomic seating. Unlike solid foam or gel pads which bottom out under body weight, our cushion utilizes interconnected air-cell columns. When you sit, air flows from cell to cell to match your unique pelvic bone structure, distributing load vectors evenly and preventing points of concentrated stress.

This clinical weight distribution decreases tailbone fatigue, allows vascular blood flow to remain unobstructed, and limits compression of the sciatic nerve. It is highly recommended for office professionals, long-distance drivers, post-op tailbone surgery patients, and full-time wheelchair users.

How to Inflate & Adjust:

  1. Twist the brass valve counter-clockwise to open the seal.
  2. Attach the rubber nozzle of the included pump to the valve.
  3. Pump air until the cushion is fully expanded (around 20 pumps). We recommend inflating to 70% capacity rather than 100% stiffness for the best contour immersion.
  4. Close the valve tightly by twisting clockwise. Sit down; if you feel the seat bottom below, open the valve slightly, add 3-5 pumps of air, and close.

Washing Instructions:

  • Unzip and remove the outer mesh cover. Machine wash the cover in cold water on a gentle cycle. Air dry or tumble dry on low.
  • Do not wash the PVC cushion insert in a washing machine. Clean the PVC insert by wiping it down with a damp cloth and mild soap. Air dry completely before re-inserting into the cover.
