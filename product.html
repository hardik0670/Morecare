Inflation & Adjustment

  1. Adjust or inflate as per product instructions included in the box.
  2. For cushion products: inflate to 70% capacity for optimal contour support.
  3. For pillows: allow 24-48 hours for memory foam to fully expand after unboxing.

Washing Instructions

  • Remove the outer cover. Machine wash on a gentle cycle in cold water.
  • Air dry or tumble dry on low heat. Do not bleach.
  • Wipe the inner core with a damp cloth and mild soap. Air dry completely.
