Do you offer CP-specific pediatric products?
Absolutely! Our CP Kids range includes pediatric power wheelchairs, tilt-in-space chairs, standing frames, gait trainers, and specialized therapy aids – all designed for children with cerebral palsy.
Can CP standing frames be adjusted as my child grows?
Yes, all Morecare CP standing frames, gait trainers, and wheelchairs are built with growth-adaptable frames. The height, chest supports, knee pads, and footplates are fully adjustable to grow with your child over 3-4 years.
Do you offer customized sizing for children's wheelchairs?
Yes. We understand that every child has unique anatomical and postural needs. We provide customization for seat width, depth, backrest angle, lateral thoracic supports, and head support based on clinical therapist recommendations.
Are Morecare products medically approved?
Yes, our manufacturing facility is ISO 13485 certified for medical devices, and our flagship products are registered with relevant medical authorities to meet global rehabilitation criteria.
