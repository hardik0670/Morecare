Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

Learn more about our trial periods, shipping policies, warranty covers, and clinical testings.

Do Morecare seat cushions fit on any office chair?
Yes, our cushions are designed to fit standard office chairs, car seats, wheelchairs, and sofas. The anti-slip silica grip base ensures the cushion stays locked in place on fabric, leather, or mesh surfaces without shifting.
What is the return or trial policy for cushions?
We offer a 30-day, risk-free trial for all ergonomic cushions and sleep contour pillows. If you do not experience structural relief from tailbone or neck pains, you can request a return for a full refund, no questions asked.
How does the Carbon X wheelchair perform on outdoor terrain?
The Morecare Carbon X is equipped with solid puncture-proof rear tires and high-grade front shocks. It is designed to navigate ramps, sidewalks, grass, and gravel paths smoothly. The electromagnetic braking engages automatically the second you release the joystick, protecting against slopes.
Are Morecare products approved by medical agencies?
Yes, our manufacturing facility is ISO 13485 certified for medical devices, and our flagship cushions and carbon electric wheelchairs are registered with the FDA, meeting strict rehabilitation criteria.
