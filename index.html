FAQs

Got questions? We have answers.

Find quick answers to common questions about Morecare products, orders, shipping, warranty and more.

Can I customize a Morecare product?
Yes. Every product can be tailored — seat width, height, frame color, accessories, or specific patient dimensions. Our design team works with therapists and caregivers.
How do I request customization?
Via 'Send Enquiry' on product page, WhatsApp (+91 63775 09047), or email contactus@morecare.in. Team sends customized quotation, design drawing, or demo video.
What is the delivery time?
Ready products: 3–5 business days. Customized products: 7–15 days. All orders dispatched within 12–24 hours of completion with shipping insurance.
How will my product be delivered?
DTDC/TACKON for small shipments; VRL Logistics for large/heavy items (beds, lifts, walkers). Packed with anti-skid and protective materials.
What payment methods do you accept?
UPI / Net Banking / Credit & Debit Cards, NEFT / RTGS Bank Transfer, Cash (in-store only). Payments through secure, verified gateways.
What is the warranty?
Electric lifts and beds: 3 years. Manual frames and mobility aids: 1 year. Covers manufacturing defects and motor/structural components (not wear-and-tear or misuse).
Is after-sales support available?
Yes — spare parts, accessories, repairs always available. Replacement parts (motors, belts, wheels), video or in-person servicing, annual maintenance contracts for institutions.
How do I track my order?
Tracking ID sent via WhatsApp and email once shipped. Team also monitors delivery until it reaches your doorstep.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, to select international destinations. Delivery timelines and freight charges vary by country. Contact export support team for quotations.
What if my product arrives damaged?
Every shipment is insured and video-recorded during dispatch. Take a video and share within 24 hours — team will replace or repair per warranty conditions.
Why should I trust Morecare?
Manufacturer-direct quality (designed and produced in Jaipur). Therapist-approved. Transparent service (real humans, no bots). Insured delivery. Customer-first approach.
How can I get support?
WhatsApp +91 63775 09047 or email contactus@morecare.in. Support: Mon–Sat, 9 AM–6 PM.
