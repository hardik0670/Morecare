Frequently Asked Questions

Learn about trial periods, shipping, warranties, and clinical testing.

Do Morecare cushions fit on any chair?
Yes, our cushions fit standard office chairs, car seats, wheelchairs, and sofas. The anti-slip silica grip base ensures the cushion stays locked.
What is the return or trial policy?
We offer a 30-day, risk-free trial for all ergonomic cushions and pillows. Return for a full refund, no questions asked.
Are Morecare products medically approved?
Yes, our manufacturing is ISO 13485 certified and our flagship products are FDA registered, meeting strict rehabilitation criteria.
Do you offer CP-specific pediatric products?
Absolutely! Our CP Kids range includes pediatric power wheelchairs, tilt-in-space chairs, standing frames, gait trainers, and specialized therapy aids – all designed for children with cerebral palsy.
